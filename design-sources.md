# Akibit visual direction

The user requested refined Japanese-inspired styling with kintsugi veins of gold, plus an optional dark mode that becomes gaming-inspired. This is a design influence, not a claim that Akibit is based in Japan or produces traditional Japanese crafts.

The Kintsugi Labo JAPAN describes kintsugi as repairing vessels with urushi lacquer and preserving their story: https://kintsugilabo.com/. This informed restrained, irregular repair seams rather than generic lightning graphics. The website does not reuse the craft source's photography.

Two original conceptual hero images were requested as a single batch: an ivory ceramic ring with gold seams for Studio mode, and an angular obsidian ring with cyan/violet lighting for Arcade mode. Managed source paths are /manus-storage/async-images/RMovyTZsZL6hKDd8dFYxhg/image-1.webp and /manus-storage/async-images/RMovyTZsZL6hKDd8dFYxhg/image-2.webp. They must be exported and verified as finished bytes before inclusion in Netlify. Pending or failed generation graphics are not final assets.

Space Grotesk is self-hosted from Google Fonts, with its SIL Open Font License stored alongside the existing self-hosted DM Sans and Instrument Serif licences. No runtime Google Fonts request is needed. Decorative code-based seam paths and the small fractured-A mark remain source-controlled interface motifs.

The final hero assets were exported and visually verified as 1536 × 1920 WebP images, then included in client/public/art for Netlify. Their full subject framing was preserved; no generated placeholder bytes are published.
